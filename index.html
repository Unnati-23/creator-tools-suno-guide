For video generation

Seedance 2.5

02

ByteDance's flagship video model. Write the scene, drop in your references and get cinematic, multi-shot clips with sound, ready to cut into a reel.

  • Multi-shot clips up to 30 seconds, with audio generated in the same pass.
  • Up to 50 reference images or clips to lock in your character, product or look.
  • Fix one region, like a face or an object, without regenerating the whole clip.

For editing your videos with AI

Palmier Pro

03

An open-source video editor for Mac that Claude Code can drive. Connect it once and your agent trims, splits, reorders and generates clips right on your timeline while you watch.

  • Full multi-track editor for video, audio, images and text, free with no account.
  • Works with Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Codex and Cursor over MCP.
  • Generate Seedance and Kling clips inside the timeline instead of bouncing between apps.

For smooth screen recordings

Recordly

04

A free, open-source screen recorder that makes your demos look produced. It follows your cursor and adds the zooms and camera movement for you, so tutorials feel smooth instead of static.

  • Automatic zoom suggestions based on where your cursor is working.
  • Smooth cursor motion, click effects, webcam overlay and auto-captions.
  • Styled frames, backgrounds and a timeline editor, then export to MP4 or GIF.
